Privacy Policy

How GlowNest Beauty Studio collects, protects, and respects your personal data.

Last updated: October 2026

At GlowNest Beauty Studio ("GlowNest", "we", "us", or "our"), your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website, schedule appointments, or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our online appointment booking system or contact us, we collect information necessary to deliver our services, including:

  • Contact details: Your full name, mobile phone number, and email address.
  • Appointment details: Selected services, preferred dates, times, and optional notes or special requests.
  • Communication records: Inquiries sent via our contact form or official WhatsApp support channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate salon operation purposes:

  • Scheduling, confirming, and managing your salon appointments.
  • Sending booking reference numbers, reminders, and updates regarding your appointments.
  • Responding to inquiries, service feedback, and customer support queries.
  • Maintaining appointment histories to provide personalized customer service on future visits.

3. Information Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal data to any third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is stored securely on protected servers and accessible only by authorized salon administration and staff.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your personal data stored in our system at any time by contacting us directly at {{ business_email }} or visiting our front desk.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please reach out via phone at {{ business_phone }} or by email at {{ business_email }}.
